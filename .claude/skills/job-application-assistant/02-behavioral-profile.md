## Overview
Brendan's self-assessed working style is that of an independent, deliberate builder who thrives in small, fast-iterating teams with strong mentorship nearby. He does his best work owning a piece of a project end-to-end and syncing at natural milestones, rather than through constant oversight or rigid process.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Autonomy | High | Prefers owning work independently, syncing at milestones rather than needing constant oversight |
| Pace & Iteration | High | Thrives in fast, iterative build-test-iterate environments; comfortable with ambiguity |
| Structure/Process | Low-Moderate | Not energized by rigid process or heavy hierarchy; prefers small, hands-on teams over bureaucracy |
| Deliberation | High | Gathers information and thinks an approach through before committing, rather than moving on instinct alone |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Deliberate, thoughtful decision-making:** Takes time to think through an approach before committing, balanced with comfort in fast-iterating environments once a direction is set.
- **Independent ownership:** Prefers to own a piece of work and drive it forward solo, syncing with the team at natural checkpoints.
- **Relationship-focused communication:** Builds rapport and communicates with warmth and context, rather than purely transactional exchanges.

## How You Work Best
- Small, hands-on teams where you can be close to the whole project, not one narrow slice of it
- Environments with experienced engineers nearby who actively mentor and give feedback
- Fast, iterative prototyping cycles with room to adjust course as you learn

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Early-career depth:** Currently building deeper product design experience beyond capstone/internship-level work - frame as active, deliberate skill-building through junior/entry-level roles, not a gap.
- **Balancing deliberation with speed:** A preference for thinking things through fully is a strength for design tradeoffs, worth pairing explicitly with the fast-iteration environments he thrives in - frame as "measured but fast" rather than slow.
